Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, and a hashed password, or your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google.
  • Learning profile: the language you're learning, your native language, your stated proficiency level, your learning goals, your daily practice goal, and optionally your gender (used only to personalize the coach persona you interact with — e.g. the "strict mode" coaching style).
  • Voice and conversation data: when you practice with the AI coach, your spoken audio is streamed in real time to be transcribed and responded to, and the resulting conversation transcript (what you and the coach said, and when) is stored so the coach can track your progress and tailor future sessions.
  • Practice and progress data: session duration, scenario and language practiced, evaluation scores (speaking, listening, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation), and the specific grammar points or vocabulary you tend to get wrong, so we can personalize what the coach reinforces.
  • Subscription and billing data: your plan, subscription status, and a subscription identifier from our payment processor, Dodo Payments. We do not collect or store your card number — Dodo Payments handles your payment details directly as the payment processor and merchant of record.
  • Technical data: standard information sent by any app or browser, such as IP address, device type, and app version, collected automatically as part of operating the Service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the practice-conversation experience itself.
  • To personalize scenarios, difficulty, and coaching style to your profile and history.
  • To track your progress, streaks, and the vocabulary/grammar you're working on.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To send you optional local reminders to practice (these are generated on your device and never involve us collecting a push token today).
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the Service, and to communicate important changes.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers ("sub-processors") who help us run Speakly, each bound to use your data only to provide their service to us:

  • Dodo Payments — processes subscription payments and billing.
  • Deepgram — transcribes your spoken audio into text during practice sessions.
  • Cartesia — generates the AI coach's spoken voice replies.
  • Google — powers Google sign-in, and some of the AI models behind the coach.
  • OpenAI — powers evaluation and feedback generation for your practice sessions.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Speakly, our users, or others.

Voice Data Specifically

Because Speakly is a spoken-conversation product, your voice is sent to our speech-to-text provider in real time to be transcribed, and the text transcript (not the raw audio) is what we retain and use to track your progress. We do not use your voice to identify you biometrically, and we do not share your voice recordings with anyone beyond the transcription provider necessary to make the feature work.

Data Retention

We retain your account and practice history for as long as your account is active, so the coach can keep learning what to reinforce. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, session transcripts, and progress data, except where we're required to retain billing records for longer for tax or accounting purposes.
